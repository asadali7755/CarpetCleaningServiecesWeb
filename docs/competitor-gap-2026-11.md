# Why low-DR sites outrank us, and how we get above them (25 Nov 2026)

Checked: the Google UAE results and Maps packs for "carpet cleaning dubai /
abu dhabi / sharjah", "rug cleaning dubai", "carpet washing dubai" (Chrome,
`gl=ae`); the sites ranking with Ahrefs DR 0-6 plus carpetprowash.com; their
sitemaps, ranking pages, schema and domain age.

## The short answer

DR is not the problem. What the winners have and we don't:

1. **A Google Business Profile whose main category is "Carpet cleaning service".**
   Every business in the Maps pack for all 5 keywords has that category,
   including small ones (Golden Care 24 reviews, Al Mamzar 24, Al Deira 9).
   Our profile, *Madinat Alhaya Building Cleaning Services*, is
   **"House cleaning service", 4.2 stars from 77 reviews, website
   servedubai.ae**. So we never enter the carpet Maps pack, and the "near me"
   searches (1,693 impressions) go to the pack.
2. **One page per service + city / area, with the city in the URL.**
   carpetsolutions.ae (DR 0) has only 21 pages, but 10 of them are
   service + Dubai pages: `/carpet/carpet-cleaning-dubai/`,
   `/persian-carpet-cleaning-dubai/`, `/stain-removal-dubai/`,
   `/office-carpet-cleaning-dubai/`. carpetprowash.com has 23 such pages
   (`/carpet-cleaning-services-dubai-marina`, `/carpet-cleaning-abu-dhabi`…).
   We have 4 (our service URLs have no city in them).
3. **A real address, a map and LocalBusiness address / geo schema on the page.**
   thecleanfox, gbfm, grandlaundryhub, churchill, evershine, carpetprowash all
   show a street address; carpetprowash, grandlaundryhub and primegreen add
   `PostalAddress` + `GeoCoordinates` in schema; churchill and carpetprowash
   embed a map. Our pages show only "Dubai" and no map on the home page.
4. **Reviews.** Pack businesses have 24 to 7,900 reviews at 4.5-5.0. We have
   77 at 4.2, mostly about house cleaning.
5. **City-focused content clusters.** gbfm.ae ranks in Abu Dhabi with
   several Abu Dhabi carpet-shampoo articles; grandlaundryhub.ae is all about
   Abu Dhabi and has Arabic `/ar` pages. Our Abu Dhabi presence is one page.

Not the reason: domain age (ours 2021; primegreencleaning.com is from April
2026 and ranks), word count (ours is in range), speed (ours is fast).
